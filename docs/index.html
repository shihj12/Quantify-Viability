What it does

Guided workflow Load → Tune → Review → Export, fully keyboard-driven.
Auto-threshold MaxEntropy starting point; nudge with the arrow keys.
Live measurements Positive pixels, area %, mean & integrated intensity.
Highlight marks Outline aid makes small blobs easy to see while tuning.
Exclude regions Mask out scale bars, text and debris from the count.
Three exports Excel results, annotated PNGs, and a before/after QC PDF.
